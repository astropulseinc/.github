Keep the AI infrastructure product and runtime that make you different. **Astro Platform** supplies production deployment, networking, governance and operations in your cloud. **Nova** helps build and operate it.

### Astro Platform — the production foundation
The production control plane for teams building AI infrastructure products. Keep your runtime and product logic while Astro Platform handles Kubernetes, deployment, networking, lifecycle and governance — on EKS, GKE, AKS, or your own clusters.

- **Model serving** — deploy open models (e.g. from Hugging Face) as applications, with sizing, scaling and exposure built in
- **Managed add-ons** — ingress, automatic HTTPS and DNS, cert-manager, vulnerability scanning, cost visibility, accelerator metrics
- **Bring your own cluster** — register any Kubernetes cluster and manage it from the console, `astroctl`, the API, or the Python SDK

### Nova — the trust layer for AI operations

<img src="https://astropulse.io/images/nova-selection.png" alt="Nova — Deploy, Investigate, Operate" width="100%" />

Nova is the AI platform engineer for building, deploying and operating AI infrastructure — with durable plans, skill-driven workflows, evidence-based investigations and governed operations. Use it as a managed cloud workspace or straight from your AI editor.
